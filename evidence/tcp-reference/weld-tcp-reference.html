Use A as the distance reference

B is the current virtual TCP. It is defined in the model relative to A, rather than by a mark on the physical camera.

To measure from the J6 tool-frame origin, combine its fixed CAD transform with the measured hand–eye transform. Then convert the camera’s measured 3D point into that frame:

T_J6_camera = T_J6_weld_tcp × T_weld_tcp_camera p_J6 = T_J6_camera × p_camera distance from A = length(p_J6.xyz)

For a physical flange-face datum, confirm that face’s center and its fixed offset to A. The model defines A at the J6 tool-frame origin; the image does not independently measure the metal face. Scene-point distance also needs depth, such as a known plane or calibrated laser plane.
